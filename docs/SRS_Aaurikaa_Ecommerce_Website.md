# Scope of Work

## Homepage & Visual Merchandising

The homepage will provide a premium, mobile-first shopping experience designed to introduce the brand, showcase products and guide customers towards relevant collections and shopping destinations.

### Key Features

- Announcement and promotional messaging
- Main navigation and search
- Hero campaign section
- New Arrivals
- Featured and bestselling products
- Category discovery
- Collection Stories
- Shop by Occasion
- Promotional campaign banners
- Brand Story
- Shop the Look
- Styled by You / UGC
- Customer trust and shipping messaging
- Customer engagement section
- Footer navigation and business information

The homepage content and promotional elements will be manageable through the administrative platform where applicable.

## Product Catalogue & Discovery

The platform will allow customers to browse and discover jewellery through structured categories, collections and search.

### Key Features

- Product listing
- Category-based browsing
- Collection-based browsing
- Occasion-based discovery
- Product search
- Product filtering
- Product sorting
- Product availability indicators
- Product cards
- Quick Add functionality
- Related product recommendations
- New Arrivals
- Bestsellers
- Curated product showcases

The discovery experience will be responsive and optimized for both mobile and desktop customers.

## Product Detail & Product Media

Each product will have a dedicated Product Detail Page providing customers with the information required to evaluate and purchase the product.

### Key Features

- Product name and description
- Product pricing
- Discounted pricing where applicable
- Product SKU
- Product images and gallery
- Product video where applicable
- Product variants
- Variant-specific pricing
- Variant availability
- Quantity selection
- Product features and additional information
- Add to Bag
- Wishlist
- Related products
- Product availability status
- Product SEO information

The system will support product-level and variant-level information without making jewellery-specific attributes mandatory for every product.

## Categories, Collections & Occasions

The platform will provide curated discovery mechanisms allowing customers to explore jewellery according to product categories, collections and occasions.

### Key Features

- Category browsing
- Category hierarchy
- Curated collections
- Occasion-based shopping
- Product association with collections
- Product association with occasions
- Collection and occasion visibility
- Ordering of curated content
- Relevant SEO information

Typical occasion-based destinations may include Wedding, Festive, Party and Everyday jewellery.

## Search

The search functionality will help customers quickly locate relevant products from the catalogue.

### Key Features

- Product search
- Search by product name or relevant catalogue information
- Search results page
- Search result filtering
- Search result sorting
- Product availability visibility
- Direct navigation to Product Detail Pages

## Shop the Look

The Shop the Look experience will allow the business to present curated jewellery combinations and styled product recommendations.

### Key Features

- Curated look creation
- Look imagery
- Mobile-specific imagery where applicable
- Look title and supporting content
- Associated products
- Product links
- Call-to-action
- Display ordering
- Visibility management

This will allow customers to discover complete jewellery styling ideas rather than viewing individual products in isolation.

## Styled by You / UGC

The storefront will provide a curated social-content section for showcasing customer and creator content.

### Key Features

- Image or video content
- Customer/creator attribution
- Caption or supporting text
- Product association where applicable
- Optional external/social link
- Display ordering
- Enable/disable controls
- Content deletion

The initial implementation will be a curated content management system and will not include automated Instagram or other social-media feed integration.

## Wishlist

Customers will be able to save products for future consideration.

### Key Features

- Add product to Wishlist
- Remove product from Wishlist
- View saved products
- Product availability visibility
- Move/add available products to the shopping bag

## Shopping Cart & Mini Cart

The Cart module will allow customers to review and manage products selected for purchase.

### Key Features

- Add to Bag
- Quick Add
- Mini Cart
- Cart page
- Quantity modification
- Remove products
- Variant-aware cart items
- Subtotal calculation
- Coupon application
- Shipping calculation
- Order summary
- Continue Shopping
- Proceed to Checkout

## Checkout & Payments

The Checkout module will provide customers with a structured process for completing purchases.

### Key Features

- Customer information
- Shipping address
- Billing information where applicable
- Delivery option selection
- Shipping charge calculation
- Coupon application
- GST/tax calculation
- Order summary
- Cash on Delivery
- PhonePe payment
- Payment status
- Order placement
- Order confirmation

Payment processing will be handled through the applicable integrated payment mechanism and will not require the ecommerce platform to store sensitive payment credentials.

## Customer Account & Email OTP Authentication

Customers will have access to a secure account area for managing their personal information and ecommerce activity.

### Key Features

- Customer registration
- Customer login
- Email-based OTP verification
- Password recovery
- Logout
- Profile management
- Address management
- Order history
- Order details
- Invoice access
- Return and replacement status
- Refund status
- Wishlist access

Email OTP will be used for customer verification and authentication. SMS OTP and WhatsApp authentication are not included in the current scope.

## Order Management & Order Tracking

The platform will provide customers with visibility of their orders from placement through fulfilment and delivery.

### Key Features

- Order creation
- Unique order number
- Order summary
- Order item details
- Pricing and discount information
- Payment information
- Shipping information
- Order status
- Order cancellation where applicable
- Order history
- Invoice access
- Shipment information
- Courier information
- AWB/tracking number
- Tracking timeline
- Delivery status

Customers will be able to track relevant order and shipment progress from their account.

## Reviews & Ratings

Customers will be able to provide feedback on products they have purchased.

### Key Features

- Product ratings
- Customer reviews
- Review submission
- Review management
- Review moderation
- Rating summary
- Review media where applicable

Reviews and ratings will help customers make informed purchasing decisions while providing the business with customer feedback.

## Shipping & Shiprocket Integration

The platform will integrate with Shiprocket to support order fulfilment and shipment tracking.

### Key Features

- Shipping charge calculation
- Shipment creation
- Courier assignment
- AWB generation
- Shipment tracking
- Shipment status updates
- Forward shipment management
- Customer-facing tracking
- Return/reverse shipment support
- Return pickup tracking
- Shipment information within the Admin Platform

## Inventory Management

The Inventory Management module will provide centralized control over product and variant stock.

### Key Features

- Product-level inventory
- Variant-level inventory
- Available stock
- Low-stock identification
- Out-of-stock identification
- Manual stock adjustment
- Stock adjustment reason
- Stock movement/history
- Inventory visibility within product management
- Low-stock monitoring
- Inventory reporting

The inventory lifecycle will also support appropriate stock updates resulting from orders, cancellations, approved returns and replacement orders.

## Returns Management

Customers will be able to initiate eligible return requests according to the business's approved return policy.

### Key Features

- Return eligibility
- Return request
- Return reason
- Photo/video evidence submission
- Return review
- Approval/rejection
- Reverse pickup
- Return shipment tracking
- Return receipt
- Inspection
- Return status
- Customer visibility
- Administrative review
- Appeal handling where applicable

The evidence-based workflow will allow the business to evaluate the condition and reason for a return before determining the appropriate resolution.

## Replacement Management

The platform will support an evidence-driven replacement workflow based on the approved business policy.

### Key Features

- Replacement request
- Eligibility verification
- Photo/video evidence
- Administrative review
- Approval/rejection
- Inspection
- Replacement decision
- Replacement order
- Replacement inventory adjustment
- Replacement shipment
- Shipment tracking
- Customer status visibility

The replacement process will be integrated with inventory and shipping so that approved replacements can proceed through the normal fulfilment process.

## Refund Management

Refunds will be managed in accordance with the Refund Policy approved by AAURIKAA Lifestyles Private Limited.

### Key Features

- Refund eligibility
- Refund calculation
- Refund approval
- Refund processing
- Refund status
- Refund history
- Customer visibility
- Administrative refund management

The exact refund method, destination, eligibility conditions, and applicable timelines will be governed by the client-approved Refund Policy. The final refund policy will be confirmed before implementation of the refund workflow.

## GST & Tax Management

The platform will calculate and display applicable taxes as part of the ecommerce transaction.

### Key Features

- GST calculation
- CGST and SGST where applicable
- IGST where applicable
- Applicable tax breakdown
- Tax information in order summaries
- Tax information in invoices
- Business GST configuration

## Invoice & Order Documents

The platform will generate professional order invoices and make them available to customers and administrators.

### Key Features

- Invoice generation
- Invoice PDF
- Order information
- Customer information
- Product and quantity details
- Pricing and discounts
- GST/tax breakdown
- Shipping charges
- Total order value
- Business/store information
- Customer invoice access
- Admin invoice access

## Coupons & Promotions

The platform will provide tools for creating and managing promotional offers.

### Key Features

- Coupon creation
- Coupon editing
- Percentage discounts
- Fixed-value discounts
- Free-shipping promotions
- Minimum order value
- Validity period
- Expiry
- Usage limits
- Coupon validation
- Coupon usage tracking
- Enable/disable controls

## Customer Management

The administrative platform will provide a centralized view of customer information and activity.

### Key Features

- Customer listing
- Customer search
- Customer profile
- Contact information
- Customer addresses
- Order history
- Order details
- Invoice history
- Return history
- Replacement history
- Refund information
- Account status

## Admin Dashboard

The Admin Dashboard will provide an operational overview of the ecommerce business.

### Key Features

- Order summary
- Sales/revenue overview
- Pending order visibility
- Shipment overview
- Return and replacement overview
- Customer summary
- Low-stock visibility
- Recent activity
- Key operational indicators

The dashboard will focus on practical business operations and will not include advanced business intelligence or enterprise analytics in the initial scope.

## Product, Category & Catalogue Administration

Administrators will be able to manage the complete product catalogue from a centralized interface.

### Key Features

- Product creation and editing
- Product status management
- SKU management
- Pricing and discount management
- Category management
- Variant management
- Stock management
- Product media management
- Product descriptions and features
- Product SEO information
- Category hierarchy
- Product/category association

## Homepage & Promotional Content Management

The administrative platform will provide control over the principal promotional and merchandising content displayed on the storefront.

### Key Features

- Homepage promotional content
- Announcement content
- Promotional campaign banners
- Seasonal campaigns
- Editorial/lifestyle campaigns
- Banner imagery
- Banner destination links
- Display ordering
- Enable/disable controls
- Homepage merchandising content

The approved storefront structure will remain consistent while administrators will be able to manage the content presented within the applicable sections.

## Collections, Occasions & Merchandising Management

Administrators will be able to maintain the curated discovery structure used throughout the storefront.

### Key Features

- Collection management
- Occasion management
- Product association

- Display ordering
- Collection/occasion content
- Shop the Look management
- Associated product management

### Key Features

- Upload image/video

- Product association

## CMS, FAQ & SEO Management

The platform will provide content management capabilities for maintaining important customer-facing information.

### Key Features

- Website content management
- FAQ management
- About/brand content
- Customer care content
- Shipping information
- Return and replacement information
- Refund policy information
- Terms and conditions
- Privacy information

## Customer Enquiries & Support

The platform will provide a mechanism for customers to submit enquiries and for administrators to manage them.

### Key Features

- Customer enquiry submission
- Contact information capture
- Enquiry management
- Enquiry status/handling
- Administrative visibility of customer enquiries
- Customer care content and FAQ support

## Admin Users & Role-Based Access Control

The administrative platform will provide controlled access to operational functionality based on user roles and permissions.

### Key Features

- Admin user management
- Role management
- Permission management
- Module-level access control
- Action-level access control where applicable
- User activation/deactivation
- Secure administrator authentication
- Controlled access to sensitive business operations

## Store & Business Settings

Centralized settings will allow administrators to manage key business and ecommerce configurations.

### Key Features

- Store information
- Business address
- Contact information
- GST information
- Shipping configuration
- Free-shipping threshold
- Payment configuration
- Return policy
- Replacement policy
- Refund policy
- Shiprocket configuration
- Other operational settings

## Back-in-Stock Notifications

Customers will be able to request notification when an unavailable product or variant becomes available again.

### Key Features

- Product/variant-level subscription
- Availability monitoring
- Restock detection
- Notification triggering
- Notification request management

## Static, Legal & Customer Care Pages

The storefront will provide the necessary informational pages required for customer confidence and ecommerce operations.

### Key Features

- About / Brand Story
- Contact
- Shipping information
- Returns information
- Refund & Return Policy
- Terms and Conditions
- Privacy Policy
- Customer Care / FAQ
- Other approved informational content

These pages will support transparency around the business's policies and customer service processes.
